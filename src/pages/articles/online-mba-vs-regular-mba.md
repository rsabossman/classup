Both lead to the same degree. The real difference is how you get there and what you give up along the way.

## Recognition

An online MBA from a UGC-entitled university is treated as equivalent to the same university's regular MBA. For jobs in India and most further-study routes, the degree carries the same weight. Abroad, recognition depends on the country and the employer. See [our guide to checking validity](../check-if-online-degree-is-ugc-valid/).

## Cost

| | Online MBA | Full-time MBA |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition | Often ₹60,000 to ₹2,00,000 for 2 years at state universities | Often several lakh rupees |
| Salary given up | None, because you keep working | Up to 2 years of income |
| Relocation | None | Often needed |

## Time and flexibility

Online programs use recorded lectures, weekend live sessions and online proctored exams. Expect 8 to 12 hours a week. A full-time MBA is a full-time commitment.

## Career outcomes

A full-time MBA at a top campus offers placements and a peer network. An online MBA works best when you want to **grow in your current career**: moving into a team-lead role, switching from operations into management, or meeting a degree requirement for promotion. Many learners pick a specialisation that matches their current job, such as HR, finance, supply chain, marketing or business analytics.

## Who should choose online?

- You are working and can't afford to stop.
- You need the qualification for a promotion or a government or PSU eligibility rule.
- You want a lower-cost route to a recognised master's.

## Who should choose full-time?

- You want campus placements or a career switch that depends on recruiter networks.
- You can afford two years without income.

Not sure? Tell an advisor your background and goal, and they will shortlist two or three programs that fit.
