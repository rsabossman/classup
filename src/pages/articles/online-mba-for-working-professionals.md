**Yes, you can do an online MBA without leaving your job.** A two-year online MBA from a UGC-entitled university is equivalent to its regular MBA, needs about 8 to 12 hours a week, and has exams you take online from home. The published fees we can verify start at ₹62,625 for the full program.

## What should a working professional check first?

Four things decide whether a program will work around your job:

1. **UGC entitlement.** The university must be entitled by the UGC to run online programs. Check it on the [UGC-DEB website](https://deb.ugc.ac.in/) and read [how to check if an online degree is UGC-valid](../check-if-online-degree-is-ugc-valid/).
2. **Exam format.** Look for online, remotely proctored exams so you never need leave for an exam centre.
3. **Specialisation.** Pick one that matches the role you want next, not just the one you have now.
4. **Total fee and payment schedule.** Compare the full-program fee, not the first-semester amount.

## How much time does an online MBA take each week?

Expect 8 to 12 hours a week: recorded lectures you watch on your own schedule, optional or weekend live sessions, and assignments. Plan heavier weeks around end-term exams. If your job has month-end or seasonal peaks, check the exam windows against them before you enrol.

## Which specialisation suits a working professional?

Choose the specialisation that matches the next step in your career:

- **Moving into people management:** Human Resources, or a dual HR, marketing and finance MBA such as [VTU's online MBA (HR, marketing, finance)](../../in/programs/vtu-online-mba-dual-hr-marketing-finance/).
- **Working with data or reports:** Business Analytics, such as [VTU's online MBA in business analytics](../../in/programs/vtu-online-mba-in-business-analytics/).
- **Operations, logistics or manufacturing:** Supply Chain or Operations, for example the [University of Mysore online MBA (dual specialisation)](../../in/programs/uom-online-mba-dual-specialisation/).
- **Sales, brand or growth roles:** Marketing or Digital Marketing.
- **Finance and accounts teams:** Finance.

## Online MBA or a regular MBA?

If you want to keep earning and grow in your current field, an online MBA usually fits better. If you want campus placements and a full career switch, a full-time MBA may be worth the lost salary. Read [online MBA vs regular MBA](../online-mba-vs-regular-mba/) for the trade-offs.
