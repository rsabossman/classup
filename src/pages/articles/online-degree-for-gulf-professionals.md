Thousands of professionals in the GCC study online degrees from Indian universities without leaving their jobs. Before you enrol, it helps to be clear on three things.

## 1. Why do you need the degree?

| Purpose | What to check |
|---|---|
| Promotion with your current employer | Ask HR whether they accept online degrees from Indian universities. |
| Changing jobs or visa profession | Check the attestation and equivalency rules for your country. These differ between countries, and some treat online or distance degrees differently from on-campus ones. |
| Further study or jobs back in India | A UGC-entitled online degree is equivalent to the regular degree in India. |
| Professional licensing (engineering, teaching, healthcare) | Licensing bodies often have their own rules. Check with them first. |

## 2. Check the rules for your country before paying

GCC ministries of education and higher education publish equivalency rules, and they change from time to time. Before enrolling, our advisors will tell you what the current rules say for your country and purpose. If a program isn't a good fit for what you need, we'll say so.

## 3. Plan around exams and time zones

Most programs listed here use **online proctored exams** you take from home with a webcam and a stable connection. Check the exam windows against your work schedule and weekends.

## Programs Gulf professionals often choose

- **MBA** in business analytics, supply chain, HR or marketing
- **MCA** in AI and data science or cloud and cybersecurity
- **BBA or BCA**, if you have 12 years of schooling but no degree
