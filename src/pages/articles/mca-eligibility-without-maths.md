The MCA has traditionally required mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level. Online MCA rules now vary by university, and several offer alternative routes.

## The usual requirement

Most universities accept a BCA, a B.Sc in Computer Science or IT, or a B.E./B.Tech. They also accept a B.Sc, B.Com or B.A. if you studied mathematics, statistics or computer applications at 10+2 or graduation level.

## Alternative routes some universities offer

- **Bridge course:** you graduated in any discipline and complete the university's bridge course in computing and mathematics, alongside or before your first semester.
- **IT work experience:** some universities accept graduates in any discipline who have experience in the IT industry or a recognised IT diploma or certificate.
- **CS or IT as a subject:** studying computer science or IT as a subject at 10+2 or degree level can count.
